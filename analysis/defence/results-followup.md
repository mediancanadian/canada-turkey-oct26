## 3. Public statements by organisations

Canada-Türkiye Business Council (CTBC), FTA consultation submission dated Sep 13, 2026, posted Sep 29, 2026: https://ctbc.ca/2026/09/29/ctbc-recommendations-for-canada-turkiye-fta-negotiations/
- "welcomes the opportunity to participate in public consultations."
- Calls for an FTA that would "eliminate tariffs on aerospace, space and defence goods."
- On export controls: "past export restrictions and political uncertainties have disrupted commercial relationships"; asks for predictability "while preserving both countries' sovereign authority over export-control decisions."
- Priority sectors listed: energy (including CANDU), mining, aerospace and defence, agri-food, automotive, shipbuilding, construction, air connectivity.
- Notes that Invest in Canada said it "does not have the expertise to present on" defence and aerospace FDI.

## 4. House of Commons committee study (FAAE, 2021)

ourcommons.ca and parl.ca still return 403 to curl. The report was retrieved from publications.gc.ca (catalogue XC11-1/1-432-9E-PDF).

- Standing Committee on Foreign Affairs and International Development, Ninth Report, 43rd Parliament, 2nd Session, June 2021. Chair: Sven Spengemann. Title: "Assessing Risk, Preventing Diversion and Increasing Transparency: Strengthening Canada's Arms Export Controls in a Volatile World."
- Mandate: "the granting of arms export permits, with a particular focus on permits granted for exports to Turkey."
- Meetings Nos. 12, 22, 26, 30, 32, 34 and 40 to 43. Meeting 26 (Apr 13, 2021) heard GAC officials and outside experts (evidence PDF read via WebFetch).
- Eight recommendations, none naming Türkiye. Key ones: legal obligations as "the foremost consideration" (Rec. 1); act on credible misuse concerns and suspend or cancel permits, "as in the case at hand" (Rec. 4); stricter end-use assurances on components (Rec. 7); post-shipment verification (Rec. 8).
- Committee view: exports to volatile regions should be approached "with a default toward hesitance." It notes the Wescam sensors "was still approved for export despite the risk factors" raised by witnesses.
- Requested a government response under Standing Order 109. Whether one was tabled was not confirmed: Parliament dissolved in Aug 2021, and no response document was found.
- Supplementary opinion by the Conservative, Bloc Québécois and NDP members ("the majority of the committee"), with five more recommendations, including an independent permit review body and an embassy in the South Caucasus. It cites a September 2020 briefing note linking Türkiye's support for Bill Morneau's OECD candidacy with pending permit approvals. That is the opposition members' account in the supplement, not a committee finding.
- PDF: https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2021/parl/xc11-1/XC11-1-1-432-9-eng.pdf (record: https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.901206/publication.html)
