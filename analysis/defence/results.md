## 2. Export permits timeline (official sources)

| 2021 | 29 export permits cancelled (number stated in GAC 2020 and 2021 reports); six further Group 2 applications to Türkiye denied in 2021 | GAC 2020 report p. 12; GAC 2021 report |
| Jul 11, 2023 | PMO readout: PM Trudeau "welcomed Türkiye's decision to ratify Sweden's accession to NATO" | PMO readout |

| 2024 report text | "Since 2021, Canada and Türkiye have been engaged in a regular dialogue on export controls... Türkiye has committed to inform Canada of its intent to re-export or transfer..." and presumptive denial lifted as of January 29, 2024 | GAC 2024 report |
| 2025 | 2025 report shows no new Türkiye-specific policy section; three denials to Türkiye recorded | GAC 2025 report |

## 3. The October 2026 partnership: defence content

Joint statement, Oct 22, 2026 (Ankara), PM Carney and President Erdoğan:
- Launched the "Canada-Türkiye Strategic Partnership" with a three-year Action Plan in four pillars.
- Defence and security pillar: leaders "underlined their intent to deepen bilateral and multilateral cooperation, including in NATO, as envisaged in the Statement of Intent on Defence Cooperation."
- Cooperation for peace and security in "the Euro-Atlantic region and beyond including Ukraine, South Caucasus, and the Middle East, in particular Gaza."
- Welcomed "a Defence Sector Partnerships MOU between Global Affairs Canada and the Secretariat of Defence Industries of the Presidency of the Republic of Türkiye" to expand defence industry partnerships.
- Dialogue on mutual legal assistance in criminal matters and extradition.
- Annual Strategic Dialogue; continued Political-Military and Counter-Terrorism consultations.
- Trade pillar lists "defence production" and aerospace among priority sectors.
- The statement says the two NATO Allies "are bound together by shared values: human rights, individual liberties, democracy and the rule of law."

PMO readout, Oct 22, 2026: leaders "discussed strengthening defence ties, including greater collaboration between Canadian and Turkish defence industries"; discussed Russia's war against Ukraine, the Middle East and Black Sea security.

Statement of Intent, Jul 8, 2026: Defence Minister McGuinty and Turkish Defence Minister Yaşar Güler "signed a Statement of Intent to enhance bilateral defence cooperation," a framework covering military-to-military dialogue, operational support and a possible defence industrial cooperation agreement (DND release, read via WebFetch).

Defence, Security and Resilience Bank (DSRB):
- Declaration, Jul 7, 2026, NATO Summit in Ankara: "We, the leaders of Canada, Albania, Belgium, Greece, Latvia, Luxembourg, Romania, Türkiye and Ukraine... announce our shared intention to establish the Defence, Security and Resilience Bank." Articles of Agreement were negotiated in Montréal; aim to "commence operations as early as 2027." Türkiye is one of the nine signatory countries of this founding declaration.
- Finance Canada, Mar 23, 2026: Montréal negotiations with 18 countries (names not listed; Türkiye not mentioned).
- Reported: a Turkish official said Türkiye informed Canada it would participate "as a founding member" (Reuters, Jul 2026, via CGTN and Yahoo).

## 5. Other areas where trust has been tested

(a) Human rights and the İmamoğlu case
- No Global Affairs Canada or PMO statement on the March 2025 detention of Istanbul mayor Ekrem İmamoğlu was found on canada.ca, pm.gc.ca or via search. Reported: Canadian media (CTV, Xtra) described Ottawa as not issuing statements on some 2025 Turkish rights cases. Other governments did issue statements (for example France, Mar 23, 2025; Germany, Mar 19, 2025; EU spokesperson).
- Turkish government position (reported): the Justice Minister said the investigation is legal, not political, and that courts are independent (Hürriyet Daily News, Mar 2025); Türkiye rejected foreign comments as "prejudiced" (Reuters, Mar 2025).
- The Oct 22, 2026 joint statement names human rights, democracy and the rule of law as shared values.
- Earlier: GAC, Jul 2016, "Canada very concerned with situation in Turkey" (https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2016/07/canada-very-concerned-with-situation-in-turkey-1102179.html, not retrieved in full).

(b) Armenian genocide recognition
- House of Commons, Apr 21, 2004: Motion M-380, "That this House acknowledge the Armenian genocide of 1915 and condemn this act as a crime against humanity," carried on a recorded division. Vote 153 to 68 is reported (RFE/RL). Hansard mirror: https://openparliament.ca/debates/2004/4/21/ (ourcommons.ca returned 403).
- PM statements: Canada "officially recognised the Armenian Genocide" in 2006 (PM Carney, Apr 24, 2026: https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/statements/2026/04/24/statement-prime-minister-carney-armenian-genocide-memorial-day).
- Türkiye's MFA, Apr 25, 2006 (No. 63) regretted PM Harper's statement, called the genocide characterization "unfounded Armenian allegations," and said such statements "adversely affect the relations between Turkey and Canada": https://www.mfa.gov.tr/_p_no_63---25-april-2006_-press-release-regarding-the-statement-by-canadian-prime-minister-_unofficial-translation___p_.en.mfa
- Türkiye's MFA bilateral page: "The Canadian government's position and the resolutions adopted by both Chambers of the Canadian Parliament regarding the events of 1915 adversely affect Turkish-Canadian relations": https://www.mfa.gov.tr/relations-between-turkiye-and-canada.en.mfa
- Türkiye's general position: it "does not deny the suffering of Armenians" but "objects to the one-sided presentation of this tragedy as a genocide": https://www.mfa.gov.tr/the-events-of-1915-and-the-turkish-armenian-controversy-over-history_-an-overview.en.mfa

(c) S-400 and CAATSA
- US State Department, Dec 14, 2020: sanctions on Türkiye's Presidency of Defense Industries (SSB) under CAATSA Section 231 for procuring the S-400 from Rosoboronexport; includes a ban on specific US export licences to SSB and sanctions on SSB's president Ismail Demir and three officials; notes Türkiye's suspension and pending removal from the F-35 programme: https://2017-2021.state.gov/caatsa-section-231-imposition-of-sanctions-on-turkish-presidency-of-defense-industries/
- Canada: no official Canadian statement on the S-400 purchase or CAATSA was found.

(d) Syria and PKK
- Oct 2019 permit suspension followed Türkiye's operation in northeastern Syria (GAC 2020 report). Reported: Canada's foreign minister condemned the incursion as destabilizing (Canadian Press, Oct 2019).
- GAC statement, May 15, 2025: welcomed the PKK's announced dissolution and disarmament as a significant step toward peace; notes Canada listed the PKK as a terrorist entity in 2002: https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2025/05/statement-by-global-affairs-canada-on-dissolution-of-kurdistan-workers-party-in-turkiye.html (read via WebFetch).
- The 2021 GAC review found credible evidence that TB2 UAVs "most likely equipped with Canadian sensors" were used in Türkiye's Syria-related operations for surveillance and targeting.

(e) Travel advisory
- travel.gc.ca: overall "Exercise a high degree of caution" (threat of terrorism, possible demonstrations); "Avoid all travel" within 10 km of the borders with Iraq and Syria; "Avoid non-essential travel" to some southeastern provinces (including Hakkari and Şırnak). Page "last updated" date not shown in the fetched copy. https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/turkey (read via WebFetch).

Ukraine mediation
- PMO readout, Jul 11, 2023: PM Trudeau "thanked President Erdoğan for his leadership on the Black Sea Grain Initiative" (link in section 2).
- No PMO text specifically on prisoner exchanges was found.
- The 2026 joint statement lists Ukraine among areas for cooperation; the 2025 GAC report shows a Türkiye-origin ammunition brokering permit for Ukraine.
