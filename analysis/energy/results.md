## 1. What was agreed on energy (Oct 22, 2026)

### 1a. Leaders' statement (Official)
Source: Statement by Prime Minister Carney and President Erdoğan, Ankara, Oct 22, 2026. https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/statements/2026/10/22/statement-prime-minister-carney-and-president-recep-tayyip-erdogan

- The leaders launched the "Canada-Türkiye Strategic Partnership" with a "three-year Action Plan" built on four pillars.
- The energy pillar is "energy, and climate cooperation". The leaders agreed to "significantly advance collaboration across the energy sector, including liquefied natural gas, civil nuclear energy, renewable energy, clean technologies, and critical energy infrastructure resilience".
- They also agreed to "support dialogue between nuclear stakeholders" and to "strengthen cooperation on sustainable mining and critical minerals supply chains".
- The leaders "welcomed the Joint Statement on Canada-Türkiye Cooperation in the Field of Energy" signed by the two energy ministers.
- Canada said it is ready to work with the Turkish Presidency of COP31 (Antalya, 9 to 20 November 2026).

Readout (Official): https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/readouts/2026/10/22/prime-minister-carney-meets-president-turkiye-recep-tayyip-erdogan
- Carney spoke of Canada's capacity to support "Türkiye's energy diversification objectives, including collaboration on LNG, civil nuclear energy, and emerging energy technologies".

### 1b. Ministers' Joint Statement on energy (Official)
Hodgson (Canada) and Bayraktar (Türkiye), signed in Ankara on 22/10/2026 in Turkish, English and French. Issued by Natural Resources Canada. Copy used: Newswire (CNW), https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/joint-statement-between-the-minister-of-energy-and-natural-resources-of-canada-and-the-minister-of-energy-and-natural-resources-of-turkiye-on-cooperation-in-the-field-of-energy-892792181.html. canada.ca could not be reached from the shell. I found no copy on the Turkish ministry's English news pages that day.

Summary, section by section:
- **LNG.** The ministers agreed to "leverage Canada's growing LNG export capacity". They also agreed to "facilitate further discussions between Canadian LNG project proponents and potential long-term off-takers". Such talks "could help identify potential LNG procurement arrangements". **No volumes, prices, buyers or contracts are named.**
- **Nuclear.** They welcomed "mutual interest in exploring cooperation opportunities in civil nuclear energy based on Canadian nuclear technology", and encouraged dialogue among officials, regulators, research bodies and industry. **The text does not mention CANDU, AtkinsRéalis, Sinop, the Black Sea or a working group.**
- **Clean power.** Renewables, private investment, carbon capture and storage, and next-generation (closed-loop) geothermal.
- **Mining.** Trade and investment across the mining value chain (exploration, drilling, labs, resource reporting to CRIRSCO standards), including "battery minerals and technologies". Türkiye's adoption of the Mining Association of Canada's "Towards Sustainable Mining" standards. Possible work between the Geological Survey of Canada and Türkiye's MTA (General Directorate of Mineral Research and Exploration).
- **Multilateral.** Cooperation on energy security, the low-carbon transition and minerals supply chains.
- It is a statement of intent. No money or binding commitments appear in it.

### 1c. Earlier official step: CANDU MoU (March 3, 2026)
- **Official (Türkiye Ministry of Energy, MENR):** TÜNAŞ (the Turkish state nuclear company) and AtkinsRéalis signed an MoU in Toronto, with both ministers present. It provides for "a detailed assessment of the applicability of Canada's CANDU reactor technology in Türkiye". https://enerji.gov.tr/news-detail?id=31758
- **Company (AtkinsRéalis, March 3, 2026):** The scope covers technical data exchange, assessing CANDU suitability "for sites identified by TÜNAŞ", licensing, and "potential business models, including financing and structuring options". https://www.atkinsrealis.com/en/media/trade-releases/2026/2026-03-03-a
- **No site is named in either official text.**

### 1d. Reported, not confirmed

The same source said Ankara expected talks on LNG purchases and critical minerals. https://www.cp24.com/news/canada/2026/10/20/canada-turkiye-set-to-sign-pledge-during-carney-visit-to-deepen-defence-energy-ties-reuters-source-says/
- **The Oct 22 official texts do not mention a working group or the Black Sea.**
- Yeni Şafak (Feb 26, 2026) instead linked the Canada (CANDU) talks to the **Thrace** plant, not Sinop. https://www.yenisafak.com/ekonomi/nukleer-takvim-netlesiyor-4800897
- **Sources disagree on which site Canada is linked to.**
- Reuters (Jul 3, 2026, via BNN Bloomberg) quoted an AtkinsRéalis executive: TÜNAŞ would write an early assessment report after an information exchange that summer. https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/international/2026/07/03/atkinsrealis-anticipates-turkiyes-early-nuclear-assessment-will-be-completed-after-summer/

## 2. Türkiye's energy needs

### Energy import bill
- **USD 62.47 billion in 2025** (USD 65.59 billion in 2024, down 4.8%). Basis: HS chapter 27, mineral fuels.
  - This is TurkStat and Trade Ministry data, but I read it through Enerji Günlüğü (Jan 30, 2026). https://www.enerjigunlugu.net/turkiye-2025te-enerjiye-625-milyar-dolar-odedi-66794h.htm
  - The TurkStat bulletin site (data.tuik.gov.tr) timed out. **Treat this figure as reported until it is checked against the bulletin.**
- Total 2025 imports were USD 365.5 billion (Trade Ministry bulletin, Dec 2025). https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/695902c813b8762ee8545ef5/Aylık%20Dış%20Ticaret%20Veri%20Bülteni_Aralık_.pdf
  - Energy was therefore about 17% of all imports (my calculation).
  - The same bulletin says: excluding energy, exports covered 85.2% of imports in December 2025, against 73.7% overall.

### How much energy Türkiye imports
Source: MENR 2024 National Energy Balance (xlsx, thousand toe). https://enerji.gov.tr//Media/Dizin/EIGM/tr/Raporlar/Ulusal_Enerji_Denge_Tablolari/2024_r1.xlsx

- Domestic production: 55,016 ktoe.
- Imports: 128,437 ktoe. Exports: 12,304 ktoe.
- Total energy supply: 165,214 ktoe.
- **Net imports were 70.3% of total energy supply in 2024.** Domestic production covered 33.3%. (My calculation from the official table. Script: scripts/tr_energy_balance.py.)
- Fuel by fuel, imports are close to total supply: hard coal 24,564 of 25,150 ktoe; crude oil 31,526 of 36,786; natural gas 43,076 of 43,952.
- The MENR 2026 Performance Programme lists "Dışa Bağımlılığın Azaltılması" (reducing external dependence) as Goal 2. https://enerji.gov.tr//Media/Dizin/SGB/tr/Performans_Programlari/2026PP.pdf

## 3. Nuclear

### Akkuyu (Rosatom, Mersin)
IAEA PRIS (Official, API pulled Oct 23, 2026). https://pris.iaea.org/PRIS/CountryStatistics/CountryDetails.aspx?current=TR, data via https://pris-stats.iaea.org/reactor/reactors-by-code/TR
- All 4 units are VVER V-509, 1,114 MWe net each, and all are **"Under Construction"**.
- Construction starts: Unit 1 2018-04-03, Unit 2 2020-04-08, Unit 3 2021-03-10, Unit 4 2022-07-21.
- **No criticality or grid-connection date is recorded for any unit.** Türkiye has 0 operating reactors.
- Owner and operator: Akkuyu Nuclear JSC.

MENR 2026 Performance Programme (Official):
- Construction completion rate: 66.25% in 2024, 79% estimated for 2025, 85% target for 2026, 95% forecast for 2027, 100% forecast for 2028.
- It aims for Akkuyu to "bütün üniteleri ile elektrik üretimine başlayacaktır" (begin electricity generation with all units).

Reported, not confirmed:
- Rosatom head Likhachev (Oct 16, 2026, via SightLine U3O8 / Reuters): "physical start-up in the second half of December". That means fuel loading and first criticality. Grid connection comes after that. https://sightlineu3o8.com/2026/10/russias-rosatom-targets-year-end-nuclear-start-up-at-turkeys-akkuyu/
- The original target was 2023, and the project cost more than USD 20 billion.
- Haberler.com (Jun 11, 2026): EÜAŞ buys Akkuyu power at 12.35 US cents/kWh, with offtake guarantees of 70% (units 1 and 2) and 30% (units 3 and 4) for 15 years. https://www.haberler.com/ekonomi/turkiye-sinop-nukleer-santrali-icin-guney-kore-ile-gorusmeleri-hizlandirdi-19935827-haberi/
  - These terms come from the 2010 intergovernmental agreement. **I did not verify them against that text.**

### Second plant (Sinop, Black Sea) and third plant (Thrace)
- **Official:** PRIS lists no Turkish units beyond the 4 at Akkuyu. The 2026 Performance Programme says only that work to increase nuclear capacity "will continue".
- **Company:** AtkinsRéalis says Türkiye plans "three additional nuclear reactors".
- **Reported, not confirmed:**
  - Sinop: a KEPCO (South Korea) MoU and pre-feasibility work; a USD 30 billion, 4-reactor plan; Türkiye awaiting a binding offer (Takvim, Jun 7, 2026). https://www.takvim.com.tr/ekonomi/2026/06/07/turkiyenin-2-nukleer-santrali-sinopa-kuruluyor-elektrigin-10u-uretilecek
  - Thrace: talks with China (SPIC) and Canada (Yeni Şafak, above).
  - Reuters (Jul 2026) reported a 2.4 GW gap against Türkiye's 2035 interim nuclear goal.

### CANDU reactors worldwide (IAEA PRIS, Official)
- **26 CANDU-design units are operating:** 17 in Canada (12,714 MWe net), 3 in South Korea (Wolsong 2 to 4), 2 in Romania (Cernavodă), 2 in China (Qinshan III), 1 in Argentina (Embalse) and 1 in India (Rajasthan-2, built with AECL).
- NRCan uses the same count: "16 CANDU reactors in Ontario, 1 in New Brunswick and 9 in operation outside of Canada". https://natural-resources.canada.ca/minerals-mining/mining-data-statistics-analysis/minerals-metals-facts/uranium-nuclear-power-facts
- Other heavy-water reactors: India runs 18 more PHWRs of its own design, descended from CANDU. That gives 46 PHWRs operating worldwide.
- World Nuclear News gives "31 Candu reactors" (Mar 4, 2026). That count includes Indian derivatives. **Use 26 (PRIS and NRCan basis) and say so.**
- Total world fleet: 417 reactors operating and 78 under construction (PRIS).

  - Woodfibre: 2.1 MTPA, start expected 2027.
  - Cedar: 3.3 MTPA, start expected 2028.
  - Ksi Lisims: 12 MTPA, proposed.

### Atlantic coast LNG
- **Official:** There is no operating Atlantic LNG export terminal. No Atlantic LNG project appears on the Major Projects Office list.
- CER (2015) described East Coast projects as facing competition and supply challenges.
- **Reported, not confirmed:** In 2026 a proposed "East Canada LNG" plant of about USD 7.5 billion is reported to be nearing a decision and signing buyers (Oil and Gas 360, Jun and Oct 2026). Those pages blocked download (HTTP 403), so I saw only the search-result headlines.

### Uranium (NRCan, Official)
- Canada produced **14.3 kt** in 2024 (14,327 t), all from Saskatchewan. That was **24% of world production**, second after Kazakhstan.
- About **90% was exported**. Canada was second for world exports (24% of 53.0 kt).
- Exports go to the Americas (44%), Europe (39%) and Asia (17%). Canada supplied 33% of US reactor purchases.
- CER (Feb 2026) gives 16.9 kt for 2024. **That conflicts with NRCan's 14.3 kt, probably because of a different unit (U3O8 vs U). Use NRCan.** https://www.cer-rec.gc.ca/en/data-analysis/energy-markets/market-snapshots/2026/market-snapshot-canadian-uranium-exports-help-fuel-nuclear-generation-around-the-world.html

### Critical minerals
- **Canada's list (Official):** 34 minerals, updated 2024. https://www.canada.ca/en/campaign/critical-minerals-in-canada/critical-minerals-an-opportunity-for-canada
  - It includes chromium, rare earth elements and uranium.
  - **Boron is not on Canada's list.**
- **What Türkiye has:**
  - **Rare earths, Beylikova (Eskişehir).** MENR (2022) announced a 694 million tonne "reserve" and called it the world's second largest. It said 10 of the 17 rare earth elements can be produced there. https://enerji.gov.tr/news-detail?id=21004
    - This is a 2022 announcement of ore tonnage, not a figure for rare earth oxide content.
    - **It is not comparable with USGS reserve figures. Do not compare it with China's numbers.**
  - **Boron.** USGS (Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, a US government source, used because Eti Maden's site could not be reached): Türkiye has reserves of 950 million t, the largest of any country listed. Production was 1.8 Mt in 2024 and 1.5 Mt in 2025 (refined borates). 90% of US boron imports come from Türkiye. https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-boron.pdf
    - Eti Maden is Türkiye's sole state producer (MTA). https://www.mta.gov.tr/v3.0/bilgi-merkezi/bor
    - The often-quoted "73% of world reserves" figure **could not be verified** from an official page.
  - **Chromium.** USGS MCS 2026: Türkiye mined 9,300 kt in 2024 and about 9,000 kt in 2025 (gross weight). That is second in the world after South Africa (23,000 kt), out of a world total of 51,000. Reserves: 27,000 kt ore. https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-chromium.pdf

## 5. Benefits and pitfalls (as described by institutional sources)

| Topic | Possible benefit | Possible pitfall |
|---|---|---|
| LNG route | CER says Western Canada's location cuts shipping costs to Northeast Asia, and a cold climate lowers liquefaction energy (CER 2024). | That advantage points to Asia, not Türkiye. All 2025 and 2026 LNG Canada cargoes went to Asia (CER data). From BC to the Mediterranean, ships use the Panama Canal or a much longer route. I found no official distance or cost figure. |
| Atlantic LNG | A shorter Atlantic route to Europe and the Mediterranean is the stated rationale for East Coast proposals. | No Atlantic export terminal exists or is on the MPO list. CER (2015) cited competition and supply challenges. |
| Türkiye's gas market | Türkiye is diversifying: LNG rose to 29.3% of imports in 2025, and the Iran and Russia contracts are expiring or recently extended (reported). | Türkiye already has 11 spot LNG suppliers and a large US spot volume. Gas use in power is targeted to fall from 18.9% (2025 estimate) to 15% (2028) (MENR 2026 PP). Domestic Black Sea gas is rising (3.11 bcm in 2025). |
| Scale | Türkiye imported 58.3 bcm of gas in 2025. | Canada's total 2025 LNG export was 3.11 bcm, and capacity is committed by long-term contracts to the LNG Canada partners (contract terms are not public in CER data). |
| Nuclear | MENR says CANDU has "proven its reliability". PRIS shows 26 CANDU units operating in 6 countries. | Only an assessment MoU exists. Akkuyu shows long timelines: construction began in 2018, the original target was 2023, and physical start-up is planned for Dec 2026 (reported). Financing models are still to be examined (AtkinsRéalis). |
| Minerals | Türkiye holds large boron and chromium resources and has announced rare earths. Canada brings mining services and standards (TSM, CRIRSCO), per the joint statement. | Boron is not on Canada's critical list. The Beylikova figure is an ore tonnage announcement, not a verified reserve. Current trade is small (Comtrade). |
| Climate | Both countries have net-zero targets. Gas and nuclear are framed as transition and security fuels in the joint statement. | New long-term LNG contracts reach into the 2040s. Canada's oil and gas sector is 30% of its emissions (ECCC). Türkiye's emissions grew 5.3% in 2024 (TurkStat). |

## 6. Greenhouse gases (COP31 angle)

| | Canada | Türkiye |
|---|---|---|
| Total GHG 2024 | **685 Mt CO2e** (excl. LULUCF) | **584.5 Mt CO2e** |
| Change | -0.3% vs 2023; -10% vs 2005 | +5.3% vs 2023 |
| Per person 2024 | **17 t** (24 t in 2005) | **6.8 t** (4.2 t in 1990) |
| Largest source | Oil and gas, 30% | Energy, 71.8% |
| Near-term target | 40 to 45% below 2005 by 2030; 45 to 50% by 2035 | Cap of 643 Mt by 2035 (NDC 3.0) |
| Net zero | 2050 (in law) | 2053 |

Sources:
- Canada: ECCC National Inventory Report 2026, executive summary. https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/climate-change/greenhouse-gas-emissions/sources-sinks-executive-summary-2026.html
  - Net-zero 2050: Canadian Net-Zero Emissions Accountability Act. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/c-19.3/page-1.html
- Türkiye: TurkStat GHG statistics 1990-2024 (Mar 27, 2026), as reproduced by alomaliye.com and DHA. TurkStat's own site was unreachable. https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/03/27/sera-gazi-emisyon-istatistikleri-1990-2024/
  - The LULUCF treatment of the 584.5 Mt total is not stated in the reproduced text. **Check it in the UNFCCC CRF before a direct comparison.**
- Türkiye NDC 3.0: Ministry of Environment Climate Change Directorate. https://www.iklim.gov.tr/en/turkiye-submits-its-second-nationally-determined-contribution-to-the-united-nations-news-4640
  - The 643 Mt cap for 2035 is above the 2024 level of 584.5 Mt, so it allows emissions to rise. The previous 2030 target was 695 Mt.
- A Canadian emits about 2.5 times as much per person as a person in Türkiye (17 / 6.8, my calculation).
